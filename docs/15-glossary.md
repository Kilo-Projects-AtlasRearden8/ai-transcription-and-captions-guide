# Chapter 15: Glossary

*Part of the Free AI Transcription and Captions Guide by Asdale Dynamics, LLC*

---

## Plain-English Definitions

All terms used in this guide, explained simply.

---

## A

**AI (Artificial Intelligence)**
Computer systems that can do tasks that normally need human intelligence, like understanding speech.

**ASR (Automatic Speech Recognition)**
The formal name for speech-to-text technology. Converts spoken audio into written text.

**Audio file**
A digital recording of sound — MP3, WAV, M4A, etc.

---

## B

**Batch processing**
Transcribing a finished audio file all at once, rather than while someone is speaking.

**Beam search**
A method where the AI keeps several possible transcriptions at once and picks the best one.

---

## C

**Caption**
Text displayed on screen with a video, usually synchronized to the audio.

**Cloud transcription**
Sending your audio to a company's servers for transcription, rather than doing it on your own computer.

**Confidence score**
A number showing how sure the AI is about a word or phrase. Lower scores mean more likely errors.

---

## D

**Diarization (Speaker diarization)**
Splitting audio by speaker: "Speaker 1 said this, Speaker 2 said that."

**Local transcription**
Running the AI entirely on your own computer. Your audio never leaves your device.

---

## E

**Export**
Saving a transcript in a specific file format (TXT, SRT, DOCX, etc.).

---

## F

**Feature extraction**
Turning raw audio into numbers that the AI can understand.

**Filler word**
Words like "um," "uh," "like," "you know" — speech fillers that don't add meaning.

---

## G

**GPU (Graphics Processing Unit)**
A specialized computer chip. Much faster than CPU for AI work, but not required.

---

## H

**Hallucination**
When the AI invents words that weren't actually spoken — common in silence or noise.

---

## L

**Language model**
The part of the AI that understands grammar and predicts likely word sequences.

**Local-first**
A design approach that prioritizes tools running on your own device over cloud services.

---

## M

**Model**
The trained AI file that does the actual transcription. Different sizes (tiny, base, small, medium, large).

**MP3 / WAV / M4A**
Common audio file formats. WAV is standard for speech recognition.

---

## P

**Phoneme**
The smallest unit of sound in a language (like "k," "ae," "t").

**Privacy**
Keeping your data under your control. Local transcription is more private than cloud.

---

## S

**SRT (SubRip Subtitle)**
A caption file format with timestamps, used by YouTube, VLC, and most video players.

**Speech-to-text**
Converting spoken words into written text. Same as ASR.

**Streaming / Real-time**
Transcription that happens while you speak, with minimal delay.

---

## T

**Transcript**
A written version of spoken audio.

**VTT (WebVTT)**
A caption format for web video (HTML5).

---

## W

**WER (Word Error Rate)**
A measure of transcription accuracy. Lower is better. Human-level accuracy is about 5%.

**Whisper**
OpenAI's free, open-source speech-to-text model. Available for local use.
