## Welcome

This guide teaches you how to get free, high-quality transcripts and captions for your meetings, lectures, and videos using AI. No experience needed. Everything is explained step by step in plain English.

**What you'll learn:**
- How speech-to-text works (in simple terms)
- Free tools you can use right now — both online and offline
- Local vs cloud: privacy, speed, and quality trade-offs
- How to edit and clean up transcripts
- Privacy rules to keep your data safe
- Export formats for every use case

**Who this is for:**
- Students recording lectures
- Professionals in meetings
- Content creators making videos
- Anyone who wants searchable text from audio — for free

## A Note on "Free"

When we say "free," we mean:
- ✅ No monthly subscription
- ✅ No per-minute charges
- ✅ No account required (for local tools)
- ⚠️ Cloud free tiers have limits (minutes/month, file size)
- ⚠️ Local tools need a decent computer (see [Chapter 4](04-local-options.md))

We'll be honest about every limitation.
