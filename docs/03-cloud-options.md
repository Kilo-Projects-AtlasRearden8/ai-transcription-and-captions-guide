# Chapter 3: Cloud Options (Free Tiers)

*Part of the Free AI Transcription and Captions Guide by Asdale Dynamics, LLC*

---

## What "Cloud" Means

Your audio file is uploaded to a company's server. Their AI transcribes it. The text comes back to you.

**Pros:**
- No install required
- No powerful computer needed
- Usually higher accuracy (bigger models)
- Speaker labels often included

**Cons:**
- Your audio leaves your computer (privacy — see [Chapter 11](11-privacy.md))
- Free tiers have limits (minutes per month, file size)
- Needs internet

## Option A: YouTube Auto-Captions (Best for Videos)

**Cost:** Free  
**Privacy:** Video is public unless set to "unlisted"

### Step-by-Step

1. Go to [studio.youtube.com](https://studio.youtube.com) and sign in
2. Click **Create → Upload videos**
3. Upload your video
4. In visibility, choose **Unlisted** (not public)
5. Wait for processing (YouTube generates auto-captions)
6. Go to **Subtitles** in the left sidebar
7. Click your video, then **English (Automatic)**
8. Review and edit the auto-generated captions
9. Click **Actions → Download → .vtt** or **.srt**

### Download Caption Files

- **.srt** — SubRip format, works in most video players
- **.vtt** — WebVTT, web standard for captions
- **.txt** — Plain text transcript

## Option B: Google Docs Voice Typing (Best for Meetings & Dictation)

**Cost:** Free  
**Privacy:** Audio goes to Google; stored in your Google Doc

### Step-by-Step

1. Open [docs.google.com](https://docs.google.com) in Chrome or Edge
2. Create a new document
3. Click **Tools → Voice typing**
4. Click the microphone icon that appears
5. Speak clearly — text appears in real time
6. Click the microphone again to stop
7. Save or download the document

### Tips for Better Results

- Use a headset or external microphone
- Speak at a normal pace
- Say punctuation: "period," "comma," "new line"
- Minimize background noise

## Option C: AssemblyAI (Best for Speaker Labels)

**Cost:** Free tier ~5 hours/month  
**Privacy:** Upload to their servers

### Step-by-Step

1. Go to [assemblyai.com](https://assemblyai.com) and sign up
2. Get your free API key from the dashboard
3. Use their [Playground](https://www.assemblyai.com/playground) to upload audio without coding
4. Enable **Speaker Diarization** for speaker labels
5. Download the transcript as SRT, VTT, or TXT

*Note: The Playground lets you try without writing code. For repeated use, you'll need the API.*

---

## Option D: Otter.ai (Best for Meeting Notes)

**Cost:** 600 minutes/month free  
**Privacy:** Upload to their servers

### Step-by-Step

1. Go to [otter.ai](https://otter.ai) and sign up
2. Upload an audio file or record live
3. Otter transcribes and identifies speakers
4. Edit the transcript in their web editor
5. Export as TXT, PDF, SRT, or DOCX

## Privacy Warning for Cloud Options

When you use cloud transcription:
- Your audio is sent to a third-party server
- It may be stored temporarily or longer
- Free tier terms can change

**Do not use cloud tools for:**
- Patient health information (HIPAA concerns)
- Confidential business meetings
- Personal/financial data
- Anything you wouldn't upload to email

Use **local tools** (Chapter 4) for sensitive audio.

## Try It: Transcribe a Short Clip

1. Record a 30-second voice memo on your phone
2. Upload to YouTube Studio as unlisted
3. Wait ~5 minutes for auto-captions
4. Download the .vtt file
5. Open it in a text editor — you now have a transcript

## Key Takeaways

1. **Cloud = easy but not private** — your audio leaves your device
2. **YouTube auto-captions** are free and good for videos
3. **Google Docs Voice Typing** is great for real-time dictation
4. **AssemblyAI** and **Otter** give speaker labels but need accounts
5. **Free tiers have limits** — track your monthly minutes
