Chapter 6

Transcribing a Meeting (Cloud)

Free AI Transcription and Captions Guide · Asdale Dynamics, LLC

Scenario

You recorded a 30-minute team meeting. You want a transcript with speaker labels, action items, and a clean export.

Tool: Otter.ai (free tier: 600 minutes/month)

Alternative: AssemblyAI (free tier: ~5 hours/month)

Step-by-Step with Otter.ai

Step 1: Record or Upload

Option A: Record live

  1. Go to otter.ai and sign up (free)
  2. Click Record in the web app or mobile app
  3. Let the meeting run — Otter transcribes in real time
  4. Click Stop when done

Option B: Upload a recording

  1. Click Import → Audio/Video file
  2. Select your meeting recording (MP3, WAV, M4A, MP4)
  3. Wait for processing (usually 2–5 minutes for 30 minutes)

Step 2: Review Speaker Labels

Otter attempts to identify speakers: "Speaker 1," "Speaker 2," etc. Click a speaker label to rename it (e.g., "Sarah," "Manager").

Step 3: Edit the Transcript

  1. Click any word to edit it
  2. Fix misheard names, jargon, or numbers
  3. Add punctuation where missing
  4. Highlight action items

Step 4: Export

  1. Click the ... (more) menu → Export
  2. Choose format: TXT, PDF, SRT, or DOCX
  3. Save to your computer

Step-by-Step with AssemblyAI Playground

  1. Go to assemblyai.com and sign up
  2. Copy your API key from the dashboard
  3. Go to the Playground
  4. Drag your meeting audio into the Playground
  5. Enable Speaker Diarization
  6. Click Transcribe
  7. Click Download → choose SRT, VTT, JSON, or TXT

Meeting Transcription Tips

Before the meeting

  • Test your microphone
  • Use a headset or conference mic, not laptop speakers
  • Position the mic equidistant from all speakers
  • Do a 30-second test recording and play it back

During the meeting

  • Ask speakers to say their name before speaking the first time
  • Request one person speaks at a time
  • Spell out unusual names or acronyms
  • Mention the date and meeting topic aloud

After the meeting

  • Edit within 24 hours (while details are fresh)
  • Fix speaker names first, then misheard words
  • Add action items and decisions at the top
  • Export and share with attendees

Privacy Warning (Important)

Meeting transcripts often contain client names, business strategy, salary discussion, and personal information.

Never upload to cloud transcription: patient health information (HIPAA risk), confidential business plans, personal financial data, anything under NDA.

If your meeting is confidential, use local tools (Chapter 4) instead.

Try It: Transcribe a Practice Meeting

  1. Record a 5-minute conversation with a friend
  2. Upload to Otter.ai free tier
  3. Rename "Speaker 1" and "Speaker 2" to real names
  4. Fix 3–5 misheard words
  5. Export as TXT
  6. Read the transcript — notice what the AI got right and wrong

Key Takeaways

  • Cloud meeting tools = Otter.ai, AssemblyAI, free tiers available
  • Speaker labels are a cloud strength (local needs extra setup)
  • Edit within 24 hours — details fade fast
  • Export TXT, SRT, or DOCX depending on your needs
  • Confidential meetings = local only — privacy first
