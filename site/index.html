Get Started in 5 Minutes

  1. Record audio on your phone
  2. Transfer it to your computer
  3. Run: pip install faster-whisper
  4. Run: python -m faster_whisper transcribe file.mp3 --model base --output_format srt,txt
  5. Open the .txt file — your transcript is ready

Sensitive audio? Use local transcription (Chapter 4). Your audio never leaves your computer.

Privacy First

If you wouldn't email the audio file, don't upload it to a cloud transcription service. Patient health information, confidential business details, and personal data belong on your own computer — not a third-party server.

Local transcription = privacy by default.
