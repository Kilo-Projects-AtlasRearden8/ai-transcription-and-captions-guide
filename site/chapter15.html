Glossary

Free AI Transcription and Captions Guide · Asdale Dynamics, LLC

Plain-English Definitions

All terms used in this guide, explained simply.

TermDefinition
AI (Artificial Intelligence)Computer systems that can do tasks that normally need human intelligence, like understanding speech.
ASR (Automatic Speech Recognition)The formal name for speech-to-text technology. Converts spoken audio into written text.
CaptionText displayed on screen with a video, usually synchronized to the audio.
Cloud transcriptionSending your audio to a company's servers for transcription, rather than doing it on your own computer.
Confidence scoreA number showing how sure the AI is about a word or phrase. Lower scores mean more likely errors.
Diarization (Speaker diarization)Splitting audio by speaker: "Speaker 1 said this, Speaker 2 said that."
Local transcriptionRunning the AI entirely on your own computer. Your audio never leaves your device.
ExportSaving a transcript in a specific file format (TXT, SRT, DOCX, etc.).
Filler wordWords like "um," "uh," "like," "you know" — speech fillers that don't add meaning.
GPU (Graphics Processing Unit)A specialized computer chip. Much faster than CPU for AI work, but not required.
HallucinationWhen the AI invents words that weren't actually spoken — common in silence or noise.
Language modelThe part of the AI that understands grammar and predicts likely word sequences.
Local-firstA design approach that prioritizes tools running on your own device over cloud services.
ModelThe trained AI file that does the actual transcription. Different sizes (tiny, base, small, medium, large).
PhonemeThe smallest unit of sound in a language (like "k," "ae," "t").
PrivacyKeeping your data under your control. Local transcription is more private than cloud.
SRT (SubRip Subtitle)A caption file format with timestamps, used by YouTube, VLC, and most video players.
Speech-to-textConverting spoken words into written text. Same as ASR.
Streaming / Real-timeTranscription that happens while you speak, with minimal delay.
TranscriptA written version of spoken audio.
VTT (WebVTT)A caption format for web video (HTML5).
WER (Word Error Rate)A measure of transcription accuracy. Lower is better. Human-level accuracy is about 5%.
WhisperOpenAI's free, open-source speech-to-text model. Available for local use.
